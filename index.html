Welcome to my personal website! I am passionate about applying statistical and computational methods to solve complex real-world problems. My interests span data science, statistical modelling, machine learning and quantitative research.

About

I am currently a Senior Data Analyst (Contractor) at the Australian Government Department of Health, Disability and Ageing (DoHDA), where I develop and enhance complex health workforce supply and demand models to support evidence-based policy and strategic workforce planning. My work involves statistical modelling, forecasting, scenario analysis, model validation and optimisation to identify projected workforce shortages and surpluses across healthcare professions and geographical regions.

This work supports national health workforce planning and policy initiatives, including the Independent Review of Health Practitioner Regulatory Settings .

Prior to joining DoHDA, I worked as a Data Analyst at the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR), collaborating with Dr Son Vu on data analytics.

I hold a PhD in Statistics from the College of Business and Economics at the Australian National University (ANU), where I also completed a Bachelor of Statistics with First Class Honours and a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Statistics.

My academic research focuses on statistical inference, scalable statistical learning, distributed computing and robust estimation for large-scale data.

Forecasting Australian Apprenticeship Commencements (Nov 2024)

This interactive R Shiny dashboard demonstrates the application of three forecasting approaches to Australian apprenticeship commencements: linear regression, ARIMA and Holt-Winters exponential smoothing.

Users can adjust model parameters, compare forecasting approaches and explore their effects on projected apprenticeship commencements through an interactive interface.

Data source: NCVER DataBuilder .
